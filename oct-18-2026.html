Icebreaker — Middles and Bigs Together
Middles + Bigs

-Sit everyone in a circle or gathered around the couch area.

Icebreaker Questions — go around the circle:
1.Have you ever done something you really regretted? You do not have to say what it was — just the feeling.
2.Has anyone ever done something that hurt you and then said sorry? How did that feel?
3.What is harder: saying sorry or accepting an apology?
4.If you could instantly fix one broken relationship in your life, who would it be with?
Transition to video:
Alright. We are going to watch something today about a kid who blew it in the worst possible way. He took his dad's money, wasted it all, and then came home expecting nothing. What he got instead is one of the most famous stories Jesus ever told. Let's watch.

What it covers
Chris and Joy travel back to Jesus' time and hear him tell the parable of the prodigal son. A young man demands his inheritance early, blows it on wild living, ends up broke and feeding pigs, then comes home expecting rejection. His father runs to meet him with open arms. The message: no matter what you have done or how far you have walked away, God will always welcome you back when you come home.
Key Verse
"I will go home to my father and say, Father, I have sinned against both heaven and you." — Luke 15:18

4:15 to 4:35
Video Discussion — Middles and Bigs Together
Middles + Bigs
Kendall and Gentry — Zones 1 and 2
-Gather back in the circle or couch area. Same setup as icebreaker.
-Let the conversation breathe. Do not rush through every question.
-Smaller group today — more room for everyone to talk. Lean into that.
Discussion Questions:
1.The son spent everything, hit rock bottom, and only then decided to go home. Why do you think it took getting that low before he changed?
2.The father did not wait at the door — he ran to meet his son. What does that tell you about how God feels about you?
3.The older brother was angry that the father threw a party. Do you think his feelings were fair? Have you ever felt like that?
4.Is there something you have done that you feel like God could not forgive? What would it take for you to actually believe he already has?
5.Is there someone in your life you need to forgive? You do not have to say who — just, is it heavy to carry?
Key verse to close with:
Be kind to each other, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, just as God through Christ has forgiven you. — Ephesians 4:32
Closing prayer:
God, thank you that you are the father who runs. That you do not wait for us to clean ourselves up or get our act together first. You just run. Help us receive that today. And help us do the same for the people in our lives who need it. Amen.
