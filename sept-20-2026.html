Video Discussion Middles and Bigs Together
Middles + Bigs
Kendall Zones 1 and 2
-Gather back in the circle or couch area. Same setup as icebreaker.
-Let the conversation breathe. Do not rush through every question.
Discussion Questions:
1.Why do you think Samuel kept thinking it was Eli calling him instead of God? Have you ever missed something important because you were not really listening?
2.Chris thought he was too young to make a difference. Have you ever felt that way? What happened?
3.Would you rather have God call your name three times, or just once and you get it immediately? What do you think that says about you?
4.Samuel said "Speak, your servant is listening." What would it look like for you to say that to God this week actually mean it?
5.What is one thing God might be calling you to do right now that you have been ignoring or thinking you are too young for?
Key verse to close with:
Don't let anyone think less of you because you are young. Be an example to all believers in what you say, in the way you live, in your love, your faith, and your purity. 1 Timothy 4:12
Closing prayer:
God, help us hear your voice and actually stop long enough to listen. Remind us that you do not wait until we are older or bigger or more put together. You call us now. Help us say yes. Amen.
